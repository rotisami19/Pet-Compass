## [1.0.0] - 2026-01-14

### Initial Release
Find your lost pets with a magical compass! 🐾

**Key Features:**
- **Never Lose Your Pets Again**: Track any tamed animal across the world
- **Cross-Dimension Tracking**: Find pets even when they're in the Nether or The End
- **Smart Search**: Browse all your pets with a searchable list
- **Live Distance Tracking**: Real-time HUD showing pet location and distance
- **Unloaded Chunks Support**: Scans region files to find pets even in unloaded areas
- **Visual Feedback**:
  - Color-coded dimensions (Green=Overworld, Red=Nether, Purple=End)
  - "NEARBY!" indicator when you're getting close
  - "PET FOUND!" celebration when you reach your pet
- **Achievement System**: Unlock achievements for finding distant pets

**Supported Animals:**
Works with all tameable mobs including wolves, cats, parrots, horses, llamas, and more!

**How to Use:**
1. Craft a Pet Compass
2. Right-click to open the pet list
3. Select the pet you want to find
4. Follow the compass direction!

### Supported Versions
- Minecraft 1.21.x (NeoForge)
- Minecraft 1.20.x (Forge & NeoForge)
